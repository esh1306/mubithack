Every product entering the UK needs a 10-digit code, and last year wrong ones helped add up to £418m in surprise duty bills. Meet Prue, from Precedent: a customs broker fixes a code once and says why, and Prue gets products she has never seen right, for that broker only, with a receipt for every code. 2.5 minutes: the problem, a day without Precedent, and the same day with it.
